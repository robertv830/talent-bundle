Our Story & Approach

Talent Bundle was founded on a simple principle: recruitment should never be a one-size-fits-all transaction. Having built successful hiring pipelines for organizations ranging from fast-moving early-stage startups to top 10 Fortune 500 corporations, we understand that finding elite talent requires deep domain expertise and operational agility.

Our industry footprint covers complex verticals such as advanced technology, insurance, clearance-cleared positions, and executive leadership. Across all these domains, our mantra remains steadfast: People + Precision + Performance = Customized Recruiting Solution.

By matching our service delivery precisely to your internal requirements, timeline, and company culture, we ensure long-term retention and high-impact placements.

Our Engagement Models & Pricing

Flexible structures tailored to your team's exact capacity and budget

Contingency Search Model

Best for standard professional, technical, and operational roles where you only pay upon successful hire and candidate retention.

15% - 22% of first-year salary
  • No upfront fees; performance-based billing
  • Full active market sourcing & candidate screening
  • Coordination through interview rounds to offer sign-off
Sample Contingency Agreement Outline:
Client agrees to pay Talent Bundle a fee equal to 15-22% of the candidate’s annualized first-year base salary upon successful placement. Includes a 90-day replacement guarantee window.

Retained Executive Search

Ideal for critical leadership and C-suite roles. Dedicated search team with market mapping, guaranteed deliverables, and milestone invoicing.

25% (1/3, 1/3, 1/3 split)
  • Exclusive dedicated executive search partner
  • Comprehensive industry-wide discreet market mapping
  • Rigorous leadership assessments and reference checks
Sample Retained Agreement Outline:
Retainer billed in three equal installments (upon signing, at 30 days shortlist presentation, and upon executive placement). 25% total fee against first-year compensation package.

Contractor Staffing

Flexible talent solutions for companies needing specialized skills on a project basis. We handle vetting, placement, and administrative payroll compliance.

Markup / Hourly Structure
  • Fast deployment of pre-vetted contractors
  • Flexible hours and project timeline adjustments
  • Full management of tax and compliance obligations
Sample Contractor Agreement Outline:
Agreed standard bill rate incorporating worker compensation and administrative overhead, invoiced bi-weekly on a net-15 terms basis.

Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO)

Full-time embedded recruiters to run your entire recruitment function or support major scaling initiatives seamlessly.

Monthly Retainer / FTE Model
  • Embedded recruitment professionals working with your team
  • Scalable capacity matching your seasonal or hyper-growth hiring goals
  • Infrastructure and ATS setup optimization
Sample RPO Agreement Outline:
Monthly retainer contract guaranteeing dedicated full-time equivalent (FTE) recruiting hours, subject to a 30-day notice modification window.
